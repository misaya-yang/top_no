# Deep Review: top_no / Frequency-Offset Margin Rules — and Where the 2026 Innovation Space Actually Is

**Reviewer stance:** skeptical ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR area chair + research collaborator.
**Date:** 2026-07-10. **Repo state reviewed:** `main` at the pause handoff (`7e88b68` pilot summaries committed).
**Evidence labels used throughout:** `[PUB]` published venue, `[PRE]` preprint/under-review, `[TD]` from my training data (pre-mid-2025, not re-verified today), `[DED]` mathematical/logical deduction, `[SPEC]` speculation/judgment call.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

1. **The repository's epistemic discipline is genuinely excellent** — claim stack, theory-obligation contract, `[G]`/`[E]` evidence grading, preregistered gates, fail-closed runners. This discipline is itself a transferable asset worth more than the current hypothesis.
2. **The current narrow question ("does external-corpus frequency add information beyond logit margin?") is answerable for ~20 GPU-hours, but its ceiling has dropped.** The pilot's own data say the effect has the *desmoothing* sign (rare tokens are *less* reliable at fixed margin), is large-but-censored on math, small and half-unstable on web, and is not yet separated from context-difficulty and symbol-class confounds. Meanwhile 2025–26 work has eaten adjacent territory: next-token conformal efficiency ([VACP, arXiv:2512.22682](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22682) `[PRE]`), what truncation excludes and its frequency profile ([Truncation Blind Spot, arXiv:2603.18482](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18482) `[PRE]`), and margin-vs-noise geometry ([BAT, ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01693) `[PUB]`). What is left is a real but *narrow* measurement claim — a Findings/TMLR/workshop-sized nugget, not an ICML main-track engine.
3. **"New static sampler" is a dead genre at top venues; "training-free decoding" is not.** The min-p arc — ICLR 2025 oral ([arXiv:2407.01082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01082) `[PUB]`) followed by a devastating replication audit ([Min-p, Max Exaggeration, arXiv:2506.13681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13681) `[PRE]`) — is the field's cautionary tale, and your repo's audit history is a micro-replay of it. The innovation space has moved to **risk-controlled inference decisions attached to a consumer with a measurable loss**: when to stop thinking, which branches to kill, which drafts to accept, when to escalate compute. That space is being colonized *right now* (Feb–Jun 2026 preprints), but the population-level, budget-coupled, online version is still open.
4. **Recommendation:** pivot the program to **risk-controlled allocation of parallel test-time compute** (Thesis T3 below, deep-dived in Part 5), keep the frequency signal as one audited feature rather than the headline, and keep the current paper's core as the designated fallback audit ("Is Margin Enough?", Thesis T5). Do not resurrect the frequency-offset sampler as a method paper. I give the honest failure modes and kill criteria for both.

## 1. What the repository actually established (audit of the audit)

### 1.1 Strengths worth keeping

- The reframing from "ν-sampling beats top-p" to "truncation = prediction-set construction; is frequency informative beyond margin?" is the correct falsifiable core, and `THEORY_OBLIGATIONS.md` is unusually honest: conformal validity is inherited machinery; the NP/feature-class lens is a lens, not a theorem-product; the additive offset is oracle-shaped only under the single-index condition `h(m,n)=ρ(m−g(n))`. `[DED — I verified the claimed statements are standard and correctly scoped]`
- The stress-test spec's two structural corrections are exactly right and remain binding on *any* continuation: (i) the **entropy-Mondrian control** — without it "conditioning on frequency helps" is indistinguishable from "conditioning on anything helps"; (ii) the feature-class lattice `(m) ⊂ (m,n)`, `(m) ⊂ (m,c(x))`, with `(m,n)` vs `(m,c(x))` incomparable.
- The engineering protocol (four-way splits, MinHash dedup, one-position-per-document `[G]` rows, content-addressed artifacts, suffstats/replay design) is top-decile reproducibility practice. It is also, non-obviously, the perfect substrate for the pivot I recommend: the rollout-bank + replay architecture in Part 5 is the same design pattern at trajectory scale.

### 1.2 My independent reading of the Phase-0 pilot JSONs

From `full_tune_decision_memo.json` and the four cell summaries:

- **Direction is uniform and is the *opposite* of original ν:** all four cells have `direction = −1`; at fixed margin, rarer-bucket tokens hit *less*. This is outcome (b) in your own spec's taxonomy — the desmoothing prediction of [Hewitt et al. 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.15191) `[PUB]` — and it means the economically sensible method *shrinks* rare-token margins (prunes the tail harder), it does not widen them. The original ν rule was pointed the wrong way; the repo already knows this.
- **The math effect is censored, not measured:** `_SHIFT_GRID = arange(−2.0, 2.0, 0.05)` and every non-reference bucket in both math cells sits at −2.0 or −1.85..−2.0 — the statistic saturated its own search window. You know the effect is ≥ 2 nats; you do not know if it is 2.5 or 6. Uniform saturation across *all* buckets (including mid-frequency) is also a warning sign: it is consistent with "bucket 0 (highest-mass, in OpenWebMath ≈ operators/digits/structural tokens) is a *different population*", i.e. a symbol-class/domain artifact rather than a smooth frequency effect. `[DED from the JSONs + SPEC on the mechanism]`
- **The web effect is small and the statistic is fragile by construction:** max shift 0.7–0.8 nats but rare-minus-reference only −0.2/−0.3; the reference bucket (data-driven, highest-mass) flips between halves (2→1), which mechanically breaks half-stability. The instrument — max over buckets of an aligned shift against a *data-selected* reference — has a moving zero point.
- **The permutation control is not occupancy-matched:** permuting counts changes bucket occupancy (real: 6 valid groups; permuted: 3), so the null statistic is computed on a coarser partition with different variance; 7B-math's permutation itself saturated at 2.0 via a different bucket. The `INSUFFICIENT` verdict is correct, but partly for instrument reasons rather than signal reasons.

**Instrument repairs required before any rerun (regardless of which thesis you pursue):** fix the reference bucket a priori (e.g., the modal mass-quantile bucket on `D_tune`), report the full shift profile with document-clustered CIs instead of the max, widen the shift grid with an explicit censoring flag, use occupancy-matched permutations (permute bucket labels within margin×occupancy strata), and add the entropy control from stress-spec §4.1. `[DED]`

### 1.3 The uncomfortable structural question the repo has not answered

**Who consumes a token-level 90% prediction set?** Coverage/size of `S(x)` is a proxy metric with no native downstream consumer: samplers consume distributions (then generation quality, not coverage, is the metric — and the min-p audit shows how that ends); nothing in production consumes per-token coverage directly. The consumers that *do* exist for "a set of plausible next tokens / continuations" are: speculative draft-verify loops, constrained decoding masks, branch points in test-time search, and rollout populations in RLVR and best-of-N. Every strong 2026 direction below attaches the set (or its acceptance/stopping generalization) to one of these consumers. `[DED/SPEC]`

## 2. The 2026 landscape (verified sweep through early July 2026)

Scope note: I searched arXiv/OpenReview/ACL Anthology up to today (2026-07-10). ICML 2026 camera-readies and very recent OpenReview decisions may contain items my sweep missed; treat "not found" as "not found by this sweep," not "does not exist."

### 2.1 Static truncation samplers — genre closed

- min-p: ICLR 2025 oral [arXiv:2407.01082](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01082) `[PUB]`; then [arXiv:2506.13681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13681) `[PRE]` showed the human eval, benchmark, and adoption claims fail scrutiny ("evidence… fails to support claims that min-p improves quality, diversity, or a trade-off"). This is now the reference case for why reviewers distrust sampler papers.
- top-nσ: [ACL 2025 long](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.528/) `[PUB]` — arguably the genre's last mainstream landing; already in llama.cpp/vLLM discussions.
- Theory that explains the convergence: [BAT / Closing the Curious Case (ICLR 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01693) `[PUB]` (softmax-bottleneck geometry ⇒ principled truncation), [Decoding Game (arXiv:2410.03968)](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03968) `[PRE]` (truncation-then-renormalize is first-order minimax-optimal against TV-bounded corruption of p̂). Between them, "yet another threshold" has no remaining theoretical headroom to claim.
- Measurement that indicts the family: [Truncation Blind Spot (arXiv:2603.18482)](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18482) `[PRE]` — across 8 models/5 strategies/53 configs, 8–18% of human-chosen tokens fall outside truncation boundaries; exclusions are disproportionately *rare content words* (2.9× higher for content vs function words). Note carefully: this occupies a large part of your "what does truncation exclude, by frequency" measurement story, without conformal machinery.

## 3. Direct answer: is there still innovation space for heuristic / training-free decoding in 2026?

**Yes — but the unit of contribution has changed.** Three genres remain viable; one is dead:

1. **Dead: the static threshold sampler.** Post min-p-audit, a paper of the form "new truncation rule + benchmark wins + human eval" faces a hostile prior at every top venue, and the theory (BAT, Decoding Game) says the family is already near its robustness-optimal form. A frequency-offset threshold — *any* fixed offset — is in this genre no matter how carefully calibrated. `[DED/SPEC]`
2. **Alive and ascendant: risk-controlled inference *decisions*.** Stop/continue, kill/extend/spawn, accept/reject-draft, answer/abstain/escalate. Each has a physical consumer (tokens, latency, dollars) and a natural distribution-free guarantee story. The 2026 preprint wave (Conformal Thinking, CROP, ARC-Decode, CWWI, DeepConf) confirms both demand and — crucially — that the *composed, online, population-level* versions are still open. Training-free is a *feature* here (deployable on frozen models, calibration data only).
3. **Alive: measurement/audit instruments.** Truncation Blind Spot and the min-p audit both demonstrate that careful negative/measurement papers land. Your h(m,n) instrument, repaired, is publishable in this genre regardless of the effect's sign — but at Findings/TMLR/workshop altitude unless the finding is shocking.
4. **Alive but crowded: side-information science** (what signals beyond the model's own probabilities carry decision-relevant information — external frequency, internal unigram directions, hidden-state features). Valuable as a *module* inside genre 2; risky as a standalone headline.

The strategic implication for this project: **your accumulated asset is not the frequency hypothesis — it is the calibration/audit machinery plus the discipline to run falsification-first.** Point that asset at the decision problems the field now cares about.

## 4. Five candidate theses

Ordered roughly from most conservative to most ambitious. T5 is the designated fallback (required deliverable, detailed in Part 6). My pick is **T3**, deep-dived in Part 5.

### T1 — Certified lossy speculative decoding: calibrated acceptance with task-loss control

1. **Central question.** In draft-verify inference, relaxed (lossy) acceptance rules trade distributional fidelity for speed with no statistical contract. Can we calibrate the acceptance rule — over features including margin, entropy, draft-target agreement, and token frequency — so that expected downstream loss (per-block divergence, or end-task quality drop) is controlled at a user-set level α, while maximizing accepted tokens?
2. **Genuinely new vs 2024–26.** ARC-Decode `[PRE]` bounds drift *analytically* (fixed JS bound, no calibration, no task loss); When-Is-a-Draft-Accepted `[PRE]` gives deterministic certificates but no statistics; judge decoding `[TD, ICLR 2025]` learns acceptance with no guarantee; CSD `[PRE]` is heuristic. **Nobody has put a distribution-free, finite-sample contract on lossy acceptance, and nobody has tested corpus-frequency as an acceptance feature** (drafts plausibly fail disproportionately on rare/undertrained tokens — your signal, moved to a consumer that pays in wall-clock).
3. **Theory.** Objective: max E[#accepted] s.t. E[ℓ_block(λ)] ≤ α, ℓ monotone in threshold λ ⇒ direct **adaptation of Conformal Risk Control / LTT** (say so explicitly). The one candidate for real theory: a **composition lemma** — per-step acceptance drift certificates (the KL certificates of arXiv:2606.30265) compose into a sequence-level (1−δ) bound on total variation of the emitted text distribution; proof route: per-step certified KL → chain-rule accumulation → Pinsker + optional-stopping/Doob for random acceptance lengths. Nontrivial because acceptance events are data-dependent stopping events.
4. **Empirical signature.** Acceptance-rate/speedup lift at *certified* drift or matched end-task score; frequency feature measurably shifting the acceptance ROC beyond margin/entropy (if it doesn't, that itself feeds T5).
5. **Smallest decisive experiment.** Qwen2.5-3B draft / 7B target (both already cached on your server): log all draft/target logits on 100k positions; offline-replay calibration of acceptance thresholds; report block-KL + GSM8K/MATH-500 deltas. **~15–30 GPU-hrs**, no serving stack needed for the decisive version (wall-clock claims deferred).
6. **Main reviewer attack.** (i) Per-step guarantees composed over 1000-token generations become vacuous unless drift budget is spent adaptively — the composition lemma must be sharp, not union-bound sludge; (ii) systems reviewers will demand wall-clock on real serving (heavy engineering); (iii) ARC-Decode's authors can bolt on calibration in one revision cycle — speed matters. `[SPEC: 6–9 month window]`

### T2 — Empirical-Bayes desmoothing: estimate the smoothing component of the softmax tail and remove it with certified coverage (the sign-corrected continuation)

1. **Central question.** Is the LM's low-probability tail partly a *smoothing artifact* whose weight is predictable from token frequency, so that a frequency-indexed shrinkage — an estimated desmoothing operator, not a heuristic threshold — yields strictly better coverage/size frontiers than margin-, probability-, and entropy-conditioned calibrated baselines?
2. **Genuinely new.** Hewitt 2022 `[PUB]` told the desmoothing *story* and gave a heuristic (η); BAT `[PUB]` gave bottleneck *geometry*; Truncation Blind Spot `[PRE]` measured exclusions but with no margin conditioning and no calibration. **Estimating the smoothing kernel from external counts and removing it with finite-sample guarantees — and the margin-conditional h(m,n) surface itself — remain unoccupied** (verified by today's sweep, with the usual caveat). Your pilot's uniform negative direction is prima facie evidence *for* this thesis.
3. **Theory.** Generative model: p̂(i|x) = (1−λ(x))·p*(i|x) + λ(x)·q(i), with q a frequency-tilted smoothing prior. (a) **Identifiability lemma**: with anchor sets (contexts where p* is known to exclude token classes, e.g. impossible-syntax slots) λ(x) and the tilt of q are identified — new, small, honest. (b) **Representation corollary**: the oracle retain rule is an additive margin-offset rule `m − g(n) ≤ τ` iff q's tilt is log-linear in log-frequency — this *derives* your additive family from a generative assumption instead of assuming it (upgrade of the repo's shift-representation condition, mostly `[DED]` from your own Section 6). (c) Coverage: inherited split-conformal/Mondrian, cited.
4. **Empirical signature.** Fitted ĝ(n) approximately log-linear and stable across tune-halves *with the repaired instrument*; effect survives the entropy-Mondrian control; Pareto win at δ ∈ {0.1, 0.05} against entropy-Mondrian and TS+APS; math ≫ web gradient predicted by tail mass.
5. **Smallest decisive experiment.** The already-specced Phase-0 rerun with the five instrument repairs (fixed reference, profile-not-max, wider censor-flagged grid, occupancy-matched permutation, entropy covariate) on cached Qwen-3B/7B + one different-tokenizer model (Pythia-1.4B or Llama-3.2-3B), 300k positions × {web, math}: **~10–20 GPU-hrs.**
6. **Main reviewer attack.** (i) "The web cells already told you the usable effect is ~0.2–0.8 nats and unstable — your method's headroom over C-margin is a few percent set-size at best"; (ii) the math effect may be a symbol-class artifact that the entropy control absorbs (my median expectation: the effect shrinks a lot under the entropy control — `[SPEC, p≈0.55]`); (iii) even a clean win is "a better truncation rule," and the genre is dead (Part 3) — this caps the venue at Findings/TMLR/UQ-workshop unless the effect is large and the story becomes "we located the smoothing noise," in which case it upgrades. This is why T2 is the *engine of the fallback T5*, not the flagship.

### T3 — The conformal scheduler: online risk-controlled allocation of parallel test-time compute ★ my pick

1. **Central question.** Parallel test-time scaling (best-of-N, self-consistency, deep-think population modes) burns most of its tokens on rollouts that were doomed or redundant. Can a **training-free controller** decide online — per step, per branch, per problem — which rollouts to kill, extend, or spawn and when to commit an answer, with a **finite-sample certificate that end-task excess risk vs the full-budget policy is ≤ α**, under a hard token budget, and with the certificate *maintained under a drifting problem stream*?
2. **Genuinely new.** The occupiers each hold one cell: Conformal Thinking `[PRE Feb 2026]` = single trajectory, sequential stop, exchangeable offline calibration; CROP `[PRE]` = prefix retention vs step labels; CWWI `[PUB-wksp]` = instance-level gate; CLM (ICLR 2024) `[PUB]` = static LTT over (budget, filter) pre-long-CoT; DeepConf `[PRE]`/Certaindex `[PRE]` = population-level and online **but heuristic**. **Open 4-tuple: population-level branch allocation × budget-coupled dual control × online nonstationary validity × optimality theory.** No paper found holds two of the four; you can hold all four. `[DED from sweep + SPEC on unseen ICML-26 camera-readies]`
3. **Theory** (labeled honestly). (a) *Adaptations*: offline calibration of a two-knob monotone policy family (kill schedule λ⁻(t), commit threshold λ⁺, spawn cap) via **LTT** — inherited; per-bucket variants via Mondrian — inherited. (b) *Lens theorem (adaptation of NP/Lagrangian logic)*: under a monotone-likelihood-ratio condition on the confidence signal, the budget-constrained oracle is an **index policy** (threshold in the calibrated posterior), which makes "calibrate a threshold family" the right method rather than a convenience. (c) **The new theorem (the paper's spine): two-constraint online control.** An ACI-style update on the risk knob coupled with a dual-ascent update on the budget knob achieves, for *arbitrary* (adversarial) problem streams, long-run realized excess risk ≤ α + O(T^{−1/2}) and budget overshoot O(T^{−1/2}), for monotone policy families with censored feedback handled by an audited-exploration fraction ρ (full-budget replays on a random ρ-subset). Proof route: Gibbs–Candès telescoping for the risk coordinate; constrained-OCO saddle-point regret (Mahdavi-style `[TD]`) for the budget coordinate; the coupling and the censoring bias are where the new work lives. See Part 5 for the statement and the hardest obligation.
4. **Empirical signature.** (i) Token-vs-accuracy frontiers that dominate or match DeepConf/ESC/Certaindex-style baselines **at matched realized risk**, with the certificate holding on held-out streams (the money plot: realized excess risk vs α across the α grid, on `[G]`-grade splits); (ii) certified savings ≥ 30–50% at α = 1–2pp on AIME-class benchmarks; (iii) certificate maintained under an induced drift stream (benchmark mixing / difficulty ramps) where the offline-calibrated policy visibly fails.
5. **Smallest decisive experiment.** Build **rollout banks** — 2 open reasoning models (e.g., DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B, Qwen3-8B thinking mode `[TD: both exist pre-cutoff]`) × 3 benchmarks (AIME'24+25, MATH-500, GPQA-Diamond) × 64 rollouts/problem with per-token top-k logprobs saved. ### T4 — Whose prior is wrong? Internal vs external unigram priors as tokenizer-invariant side information

1. **Central question.** LMs contain an internal frequency estimate (final-layer unigram direction; frequency-regulating components `[TD: Stolfo et al. NeurIPS 2024]`). Define the **prior residual** r_i = internal log-prior − external log-frequency. Is miscalibration at fixed margin driven by *frequency itself* (T2's reading) or by *disagreement between the model's internal prior and corpus truth* (undertrained/glitch tokens being the extreme) — and does r_i, aggregated at byte level to be tokenizer-invariant, transfer across model families as calibration side information?
2. **Genuinely new.** Undertrained-token detection `[TD: Magikarp 2024]` finds pathological tokens; nobody connects internal-external prior residuals to *calibrated prediction-set behavior* or tests cross-tokenizer transfer of the residual via byte-level aggregation. It also answers the "your external corpus is arbitrary" objection by making the object model-relative.
3. **Theory.** Hierarchical empirical-Bayes shrinkage (token → byte-n-gram → class) with a Stein-type dominance statement over token classes — **honest label: adaptation of standard EB/shrinkage results**; the contribution is the estimator design + measurement, not the theorem.
4. **Empirical signature.** r_i predicts h(m,·) deviations better than raw log n_i (nested model comparison, clustered GLM — your existing Phase-0 statistical machinery applies verbatim); byte-level r transfers across Qwen/Llama/Gemma where raw counts don't.
5. **Smallest decisive experiment.** Extract internal priors (mean logit over a neutral context battery, or lens-based), byte-aggregate external counts you already have, extend Phase-0 GLM with r_i: **~20–30 GPU-hrs** across 3 tokenizer families.
6. **Main reviewer attack.** "Interesting analysis, but what breaks if I don't know this?" — needs T2/T3/T5 as the consumer of the finding; standalone it is a strong analysis-track/BlackboxNLP paper, not a main-track method. Extraction of "the internal prior" is also contestable (lens choice changes r_i).

## 5. Deep dive on the chosen direction: T3

### 5.1 Working title

**"The Conformal Scheduler: Certified Risk–Compute Control for Parallel Test-Time Reasoning"**
(backup: *"Spend Tokens, Keep Guarantees: Online Risk-Controlled Allocation of Test-Time Compute"*)

### 5.2 One-sentence thesis

Allocation of parallel test-time compute — which reasoning branches to kill, extend, or spawn, and when to commit — can be run as an online, distribution-free risk-control problem, yielding a training-free scheduler that certifiably matches full-budget accuracy within a user-chosen tolerance at a fraction of the tokens, and keeps that certificate valid under drifting problem streams where fixed heuristics silently fail.

### 5.3 Three defensible contributions

- **C1 (Formalization + optimality lens).** A decision-theoretic formulation of population-level test-time compute: branch-level actions, a budget constraint, and *excess risk against the full-budget policy* as the contract. A lens theorem: under a monotone-likelihood-ratio condition on the confidence signal, budget-constrained oracle policies are index/threshold policies — making "calibrate a low-dimensional threshold family" the principled method, not a convenience. *(Honest label: adaptation of Neyman–Pearson/Lagrangian index-policy logic to this setting; analogous in spirit to the repo's Theorem-1 lens, and positioned the same way.)*
- **C2 (Guarantees).** (a) Offline: finite-sample excess-risk control for the scheduler family via Learn-then-Test on rollout banks — *inherited machinery, new application surface (population scheduling)*. (b) Online: a **two-constraint online update** (ACI-style risk tracking coupled with a budget dual, with audited full-budget replays at rate ρ) with provable long-run risk ≤ α + O(1/√(ρT)) and budget tracking O(1/√T) on arbitrary streams — **the new theorem** (Theorem B below).
- **C3 (Instrument + benchmark).** A replayable rollout-bank suite (banks + zero-GPU policy replayer + preregistered gates, i.e., your suffstats discipline at trajectory scale) and a **signal audit**: which training-free signals (windowed logprob confidence, entropy, margin, vote entropy, frequency-adjusted margin, internal-prior residuals) are sufficient statistics for kill/commit decisions — the direct descendant of the Phase-0 h-surface instrument, with `(margin, frequency)` generalized to `(signal, trajectory outcome)`. Published banks make every number in the paper reproducible without a GPU.

### 5.4 Main theorem (Theorem B), reasonably formal statement

**Setting.** Problems Q_1, …, Q_T arrive sequentially. For each t, a base sampler can generate up to N rollouts of length ≤ L; the *full-budget policy* π_full spends the whole budget and commits answer A_full(Q_t) by a fixed aggregation rule (e.g., confidence-weighted vote). A scheduler π_θ, indexed by a scalar aggressiveness knob θ ∈ Θ = [0,1] (parameterizing a nested family: kill threshold schedule λ⁻(·; θ), commit threshold λ⁺(θ), spawn cap n(θ)), adaptively kills/extends/spawns branches based on filtration-adapted signals and commits an answer A_θ(Q_t). Define per-round **excess loss** ℓ_t(θ) = 1{A_θ(Q_t) ≠ A_full(Q_t)} ∈ {0,1} and **cost** c_t(θ) ∈ [0,1] = (tokens spent by π_θ)/(tokens of π_full).

**Assumptions.**
(M) *Monotone family:* for every t, ℓ_t(·) is nondecreasing and c_t(·) is nonincreasing on Θ. (Enforced by construction via a conservative loss surrogate — see §5.5 — and verified empirically on banks; not assumed for the offline LTT result, which needs no monotonicity.)
(A) *Audited feedback:* at each round, independently with probability ρ, the system runs π_full alongside π_θ (an "audit"), which materializes ℓ_t(θ') for all θ' ∈ Θ on that round; non-audited rounds reveal only realized cost. Define the importance-weighted estimate ℓ̂_t(·) = (audit_t/ρ)·ℓ_t(·).
(S) *Feasibility (Slater-type):* there exists θ° and margin ζ > 0 with (1/T)Σ_t ℓ_t(θ°) ≤ α − ζ and (1/T)Σ_t c_t(θ°) ≤ β − ζ. (Empirically checkable on banks; if it fails, the theorem's graceful-degradation clause applies with a pre-registered priority order: risk first.)

**Update.** θ_{t+1} = Π_Θ[ θ_t + η(α − ℓ̂_t(θ_t)) − η·μ_t·∂̂c ], μ_{t+1} = [ μ_t + γ(c_t(θ_t) − β) ]₊, with step sizes η, γ ≍ T^{−1/2} and ∂̂c a (signed) monotone surrogate slope for cost in θ.

**Claim.** For *any* (adversarially chosen, (M)-satisfying) sequence {(ℓ_t, c_t)}, with probability ≥ 1 − δ over audit randomness:

- (risk) (1/T) Σ_t ℓ_t(θ_t) ≤ α + O( (ηT)^{−1} + η + √(log(1/δ)/(ρT)) )
- (budget) (1/T) Σ_t c_t(θ_t) ≤ β + O( (γT)^{−1} + γ ) + ρ·(1 − E[c(θ°)])  [audits pay full budget]
- (regret) Σ_t ℓ_t(θ_t) − min_{θ feasible} Σ_t ℓ_t(θ) ≤ O( √(T/ρ) ) under (M) + (S).

With η, γ ≍ T^{−1/2} the first two right-hand sides are α + O(√(1/(ρT))) and β + O(1/√T) + O(ρ).

### 5.5 Proof sketch and the hardest unresolved obligation

- **Risk coordinate.** For ρ = 1 this is the Gibbs–Candès ACI argument `[TD, cited]`: the update is online (sub)gradient descent on the pinball-type loss; a telescoping/bounded-potential argument gives a *deterministic* bound |(1/T)Σℓ_t − α| ≤ C/(ηT) + O(η) for bounded knobs. For ρ < 1, ℓ̂_t is unbiased with variance ≤ 1/ρ; replace the deterministic telescope with a martingale-difference concentration step (Azuma) — this is where √(log(1/δ)/(ρT)) enters. *Standard once set up.*
- **Budget coordinate.** Dual ascent on the budget constraint is the drift-plus-penalty / constrained-OCO argument `[TD: Mahdavi et al. 2012; Yu & Neely 2017 — cited as prior art]`: the virtual queue μ_t stays O(√T) under (S), giving O(1/√T) long-run violation. *Adaptation.*
- **Coupling.** The two updates fight when the budget dual pushes θ below the risk-tracking value. Under (S), the Lagrangian L(θ, μ) = Σ[ℓ_t(θ) + μ(c_t(θ) − β)] admits an approximate saddle on the grid; the coupled iterate tracks it with the stated rates. The write-up must include the degenerate regime: if (S) fails on the realized stream, the scheduler pins to the risk-priority end and reports budget overrun — a *feature* (never silently trades certified risk for budget), and stated in the theorem as the degradation clause.
- **HARDEST UNRESOLVED OBLIGATION — monotone embedding of vote-based answers.** ℓ_t(θ) = 1{A_θ ≠ A_full} is **not** automatically monotone in θ: killing one more branch can flip a majority vote back to the full-budget answer (non-nested answer maps). Two candidate repairs, both with costs: (i) **conservative surrogate**: define ℓ̄_t(θ) = 1{the *set* of surviving decision-relevant branches at commit time differs in a vote-changing way}, i.e., count any vote-composition change as a loss — mechanically monotone for nested kill rules, but strictly conservative (efficiency price to be measured on banks); (ii) **randomized tie-breaking/smoothed votes** — restores approximate monotonicity in expectation but weakens the pathwise (M) needed for the deterministic ACI telescope, demanding an in-expectation variant of the risk bound. I do not currently know which repair keeps both the guarantee sharp and the efficiency loss small; **this is the load-bearing open proof problem, and it must be resolved on the banks (empirically: how often are vote flips non-monotone?) before the theorem is written.** If neither repair works cleanly, the online claim degrades to "commit-only scheduling" (single-answer streams, no votes), which is still novel but thinner. `[DED + honest uncertainty]`
- Explicitly **not** claimed as new: LTT, CRC, ACI, Mondrian, NP index logic, constrained OCO — all cited as inherited machinery. The novelty is the composed object (population scheduling + budget coupling + online validity + censored/audited feedback) and Theorem B's coupled statement.

## 6. The lower-risk fallback: T5, "Is Margin Enough? A Conformal Audit of Truncation Sampling"

**Thesis.** Across model families, scales, tokenizers, and domains, measure h over the feature lattice {margin} ⊂ {margin, frequency}, {margin, entropy}, {margin, internal-prior residual}, and report (i) whether margin is approximately sufficient for the retain/drop decision (with preregistered equivalence bounds and detectable-effect floors), (ii) the per-frequency-bucket conditional coverage of standard defaults (top-p/min-p/top-nσ/η) once conformalized, and (iii) the first calibrated reproduction of the Truncation Blind Spot exclusion analysis (what the excluded 8–18% of human tokens look like *at matched margin and matched coverage*).

**Why it is safe.** Every cell of the outcome table is a finding: a large replicated frequency effect → revives T2 with evidence; a null after entropy control → "margin is enough," a clean, citable negative that de-mythologizes frequency heuristics (directly in the min-p-audit genre, which demonstrably publishes); heterogeneity across tokenizers → feeds T4. Zero dependence on winning a benchmark.

**Scope and cost.** Stress-spec Tier-0 + Tier-1 with the five instrument repairs and the entropy-Mondrian + TS+APS controls: 5 model families (GPT-2-large, Pythia-1.4B/6.9B, Qwen2.5-3B/7B, Llama-3.1-8B, OLMo-2-7B as the leakage-clean flagship) × 3 domains (C4, OpenWebMath, Stack slice), ~40–60 GPU-hrs total, mostly on hardware you already have access to. Reuses **~85%** of the current repo.

**Venue and framing.** ACL/EMNLP Findings, TMLR, or a NeurIPS/ICML UQ-and-reliability workshop; TMLR is the best fit for a measurement paper with this much protocol. It is also, verbatim, the C3 signal-audit module of T3 restricted to token level — so running it first (it is 3–5× cheaper than T3's banks) is defensible sequencing, provided you time-box it to ~6 weeks and do not let it become the flagship by inertia.

**Kill criterion for the fallback itself.** If, after Tier-0 with repaired instruments, all cells show |shift profile| < 0.3 nats *and* per-bucket coverage gaps of standard defaults < 2pp — the audit's own headline becomes "nothing is broken," which is a workshop paper, not more. ## 7. Bottom line

1. **Do not restart the frequency-offset method paper.** The pilot's sign kills the original ν story, the effect's usable magnitude on web text looks small, the genre is dead at top venues, and the neighboring measurement claims are being occupied in real time. This is the "honest conclusion that the idea should be abandoned" you asked me to be willing to give — for the *method*, not the insight.
2. **The insight worth keeping** is not "frequency helps"; it is the pair you have been circling for three documents: *decoding is prediction-set construction* + *set-level decisions deserve finite-sample contracts*. In 2026 the valuable sets are not next-token supports — they are populations of reasoning branches, draft-acceptance regions, and stop/continue decisions.
3. **Recommended program:** T3 as flagship (conformal scheduler; banks-first, replay-everything; Theorem B as the theory spine with the §5.5 obligation resolved or the commit-only fallback taken), T5 as the time-boxed fallback and signal-audit module, T4 folded in as one analysis section, T1 kept as a written-up option in case the T3 space is visibly scooped before October (they share the banks/replay infrastructure).
